The correction that matters

The earlier $1,873 scenario for Highway 247 used an older assessment. A newer broker entry gives $82,200 for 2026. The revised ~$830 calculation still depends on the assumed rate, classification and coverage of every parcel.

The number to request

Ask for the current tax bill and assessment for every parcel ID, then confirm what changes on purchase or residential use. Keep purchase taxes, legal fees, insurance, road upkeep and future construction separate from annual property tax.

The buyer’s field guide

Five tests before the romance wins.

  1. Can I reach it?

    Obtain registered access rights, an approved entrance and a winter-maintenance plan. A visible track is not the same as secure access.

  2. Can I live there?

    Ask the planning authority about one year-round detached house, a workshop, setbacks and a usable septic area on the exact parcel.

  3. Can I use the water?

    Walk the shoreline. Check depth, bank slope, exposure, flooding and the permissions needed for a dock or mooring.

  4. Can I service it?

    Price the driveway, power connection, tested water supply and sewage system. Inspect existing infrastructure before assigning it value.

  5. Can I carry it?

    Review every title and tax account, insurance availability, road obligations and an absence/caretaker plan before committing.
